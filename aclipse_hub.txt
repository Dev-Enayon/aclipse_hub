## Project Overview

Build a modern, production-ready educational platform called **Aclipse Hub**.

The platform consists of **two connected web applications** that share one backend and one database.

1. **Public Quiz Platform**
2. **Private Student Platform**

The system must be scalable, secure, responsive, and easy for non-technical administrators to manage.

---

# Primary Goal

The platform should:

* attract visitors through free quizzes
* convert visitors into paying students
* provide premium CBT exams
* allow admins to manage everything without coding
* become a long-term archive of thousands of exam questions

## Coding Standards

* Write clean, modular, production-ready code.
* Follow SOLID principles.
* Use reusable components.
* Use TypeScript throughout.
* Keep components small and maintainable.
* Include comments only where they improve understanding.
* Build with scalability in mind so the platform can support tens of thousands of students.
